JESUSSAID

John 7:6-24

Judge Rightly

My time is not yet here; for you any time will do. The world cannot hate you, but it hates me because I testify that its works are evil. You go to the festival. I am not going up to this festival, because my time has not yet fully come. My teaching is not my own. It comes from the one who sent me. Anyone who chooses to do the will of God will find out whether my teaching comes from God or whether I speak on my own. Whoever speaks on their own does so to gain personal glory, but he who seeks the glory of the one who sent him is a man of truth; there is nothing false about him. Has not Moses given you the law? Yet not one of you keeps the law. Why are you trying to kill me? I did one miracle, and you are all amazed. Yet, because Moses gave you circumcision (though actually it did not come from Moses, but from the patriarchs), you circumcise a boy on the Sabbath. Now it a boy can be circumcised on the Sabbath so that the law of Moses may not be broken, why are you angry with me for healing a man’s whole body on the Sabbath? Stop judging by mere appearances, but instead judge correctly.”

Jesus corrects the crowd for enforcing Sabbath rules while ignoring the actual good he did — a whole man made well.

Context

At the Festival of Tabernacles Jesus is in Jerusalem, controversial, controversial-loved and controversial-hated. He tells his brothers he is not going up to the feast — his time has not yet fully come — and then goes quietly. Halfway through the festival he starts teaching openly in the temple courts. The authorities marvel that he speaks with such learning without formal training. His answer: 'My teaching is not my own. It comes from the one who sent me.' Then the diagnostic line — 'Anyone who chooses to do the will of God will find out whether my teaching comes from God or whether I speak on my own.' Truth is not verified by argument. It is verified by living it. Try the way and see whether it holds. This is the Jesus Way epistemology in one sentence.
