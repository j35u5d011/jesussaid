JESUSSAID

Luke 20:24-25

Render to Caesar

Show me a denarius. Whose image and inscription are on it? Then give back to Caesar what is Caesar’s, and to God what is God’s.”

Give Caesar the coin that carries his image, and give God the life that carries God's.

Context

The trap was clean. If Jesus says pay the tax, the crowd hears collaboration with Rome. If he says refuse, the authorities have him for sedition. He asks for the coin, has them name whose face is on it, and turns the question inside out. Give Caesar what is his. Give God what is God's. The unspoken second half is what makes it a teaching and not a dodge — because if the denarius belongs to Caesar because his image is stamped on it, what carries God's image? You do. Genesis 1:27. The coin is Caesar's little empire; your whole life is God's. Hand over the metal without a fight. Then ask what you are still holding back.
