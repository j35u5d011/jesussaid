Context

Jesus is finishing the promise from chapter 14 and giving it a job description. The Advocate — parakletos again, the one called alongside — is the Spirit of truth. He will testify about Jesus. He will guide the disciples into all truth. Crucially, he does not speak on his own — he speaks only what he hears from the Father through the Son. This is a check every generation needs. A "spirit" that leads people away from what Jesus actually said and did is not the Spirit Jesus promised. The Advocate deepens the teaching, applies it to new situations, brings it back to memory when it fades. He does not overturn it.
