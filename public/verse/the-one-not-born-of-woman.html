JESUSSAID

Gospel of Thomas 15

The One Not Born of Woman

Jesus said, "When you see one who was not born of woman, prostrate yourselves on your faces and worship him. That one is your father."

The true Father is the uncreated source that stands behind every human origin.

Context

This is dense mystical language, and it does not yield easily. The one not born of woman — everyone born of woman has an origin in time, a mother, a beginning. The one not born of woman has no such origin. It is the uncreated. The source itself. And Jesus says when you see that one, prostrate yourself and worship — because that one is your father. Not your biological father. Not the tribal deity of one nation. The prior source out of which all being flows.
