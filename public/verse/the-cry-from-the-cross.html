Context

"My God, my God, why have you forsaken me" is the opening line of Psalm 22. Any Jewish hearer at the foot of the cross knew this. To quote the first line of a psalm was to invoke the whole psalm. Psalm 22 begins in agony and ends in vindication — "future generations will be told about the Lord... they will proclaim his righteousness to a people yet unborn, saying, he has done it." Jesus is not renouncing God in his final moment. He is praying the psalm of the righteous sufferer who is heard. Mark, the earliest gospel, gives us this raw cry and nothing else. Luke gives us the softer version: Father, into your hand I commit my spirit — a quotation of Psalm 31:5. John gives us "it is finished" — tetelestai, the work completed.
