Mark 1:38

That Is Why I Came

Let us go somewhere else – to the nearby villages – so I can preach there also. That is why I have come.”

Jesus refuses to be held by a successful ministry hub and moves on to the villages that have not yet heard.

Context

It is early in Mark. Jesus has healed all over Capernaum. The crowds are enormous. Peter finds him at dawn — the whole town is looking for you. Any lesser teacher would have stayed. The audience was there. The base was forming. Momentum was building. Jesus's answer is the opposite: let us go somewhere else, to the nearby villages, so I can preach there also. That is why I have come. The centre of gravity is the message, not the ministry's success.
