JESUSSAID

Matthew 18:10-14

The One That Wandered

See that you do not despise one of the little ones. For I tell you that their angels in heaven always see the face of my Father in haven. What do you think? If a man owns a hundred sheep, and one of the wanders away, will he not leave the ninety-nine on the hills and go to look for the one that wandered off? And if he finds it, truly I tell you, he is happier about that one sheep than about the ninety-nine that did not wander off. In the same way your Father in heaven is not willing that any of these little ones should perish.”

God actively seeks the one who has wandered off and rejoices in their return.

Context

A shepherd leaves ninety-nine safe sheep on the hillside to search for the one that wandered. Not because the ninety-nine matter less, but because the one is in trouble now. Jesus tells this story to say something specific about the nature of God: God is not a scorekeeper watching from a distance to see who returns. God is the one out looking. The parable dismantles the whole apparatus of blood atonement and priestly mediation before it can even be constructed. There is no ransom being paid, no sacrifice being offered, no debt being cleared. There is a shepherd walking into the rough country because he cannot stand to lose one.
