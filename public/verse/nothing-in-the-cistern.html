JESUSSAID

Gospel of Thomas 74

Nothing in the Cistern

He said, "O Lord, there are many around the drinking trough, but there is nothing in the cistern."

Many gather around the source, but the source itself has run dry when there is nothing living underneath.

Context

This little saying reads like a lament, spoken to the Lord by someone in the community — perhaps a disciple, perhaps Jesus himself. Many are drawn to the outward form of the teaching. Few have done the interior work that fills the cistern in the first place. Crowds around a trough with no water underneath is a picture of religion without transformation. It is a picture of Jesus's own following at points in his ministry — thousands gathered for bread and healing, hundreds by the end, eleven at the cross.
