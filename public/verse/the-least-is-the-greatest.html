Do not stop him, for whoever is not against you is for you.”

Context

Jesus tells them, again, that he is walking toward death. And their response — Mark and Luke both preserve this — is to argue on the road about which of them is the greatest. It would be funny if it were not so human. The teacher is walking toward a cross, and the students are jockeying for cabinet positions. Jesus does not shame them. He does something more radical. He takes a child — a small, uncredentialed, powerless human being — and puts the child in the middle of them. Whoever welcomes one of these little ones in my name welcomes me. Anyone who wants to be first must be last of all and servant of all. The whole hierarchy the disciples were constructing is flipped. The one who serves is the one who leads. The one who welcomes the least is the one who welcomes God.
