JESUSSAID

Luke 11:33-36

The Lamp of the Body

No one lights a lamp and puts it in a place where it will be hidden, or under a bowl. Instead they put it on its stand, so that those who come in may see the light. Your eye is the lamp of your body. When your eyes are healthy, your whole body also is full of light. But when they are unhealthy, your body also is full of darkness. See to it, then, that the light within you is not darkness. Therefore, if your whole body is full of light, and no part of it dark, it will be just as full of light as when a lamp shines its light on you.”

The way you see determines the state of your whole being; a healthy inner eye fills you with light, a diseased one leaves you in darkness.

Context

The eye is the lamp of the body. If your eye is healthy, your whole body is full of light. If it is unhealthy, your body is full of darkness. The Greek is telling — haplous means single, whole, undivided. Poneros means diseased, evil, or in the Semitic idiom, stingy. Jesus is not talking about optics. He is talking about the way you see. A single, whole, generous seeing produces a life full of light. A grasping, suspicious, divided seeing produces a life full of darkness. What you take in is what fills you. What fills you is what you become.
