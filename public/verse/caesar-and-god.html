How They All Heard It

Mark

Mark 12:15-17

Why are you trying to trap me? Bring me a denarius and let me look at it. Whose image is this? And whose inscription? Give back to Caesar what is Caesar’s and to God what is God’s.”

Context

The trap is elegant. If Jesus says pay the tax, he loses the crowd who resent Roman occupation. If he says don't pay, Rome kills him for sedition. He asks for the coin. They produce a denarius — already an admission, because pious Jews were not supposed to carry a coin bearing the emperor's image and the inscription proclaiming him son of god. Whose image is this? Caesar's, they say. Then give Caesar back what is his. And give God what is God's. The line has been quoted for two thousand years as an endorsement of separating politics from religion. It is not. It is a live question dropped in their laps: what belongs to God?
