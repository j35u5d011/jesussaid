Matthew 17:17-21

Faith Like a Mustard Seed

You unbelieving and perverse generation, how long shall I stay with you? How long shall I put up you? Bring the boy here to me. Because you have so little faith. Truly I tell you, if you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you.”

Even the smallest amount of real trust in God unlocks a power that human effort alone cannot access, and its absence is why the disciples' healing failed.

Context

The disciples have tried and failed to heal a boy the father brought to them. Jesus is frustrated — you unbelieving and perverse generation, how long — and the frustration is not primarily at the crowd. It is at his own students, who have watched him do this over and over and still cannot draw on what he keeps trying to hand them. He heals the boy. Then, privately, they ask why they could not. His answer is diagnostic and disarming. Because you have so little faith. Oligopistia. Not zero. Just small. And even a mustard seed's worth would have moved a mountain. The problem is not that God required more from them. The problem is that they were not drawing from what they already had access to.
