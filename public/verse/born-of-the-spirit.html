JESUSSAID

John 3:3-15

Born of the Spirit

Very truly I tell you, no one can see the kingdom of God unless they are born again. Very truly I tell you, no one can enter the kingdom of God unless they are born of water and the Spirit. Flesh gives birth to flesh, but the Spirit gives birth to spirit. You should not be surprised at my saying, ‘You must be born again.’ The wind blows wherever it pleases. You hear its sound, but you cannot tell where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit. You are Israel’s teacher, and do you not understand these things? Very truly I tell you, we speak of what we know, and we testify to what we have seen, but still you people do not accept our testimony. I have spoken to you of earthly things and you do not believe; how then will you believe if I speak of heavenly things? No one has ever gone into heaven except the one who came from heaven – the Son of Man. Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up, that everyone who believes may have eternal life in him.”

Entering the kingdom requires a rebirth of consciousness — being born of Spirit, not just of flesh.

Context

Nicodemus comes at night. He is a Pharisee, a member of the ruling council, and he wants a private conversation because being seen with Jesus would cost him professionally. He opens politely — no one could do these signs unless God were with him. Jesus cuts straight past the flattery: no one can see the kingdom of God unless they are born anothen — a Greek word that means both 'again' and 'from above.' The double meaning is the whole teaching. Nicodemus takes it literally — how can a grown man be born? Jesus explains: flesh gives birth to flesh, spirit to spirit. There are two levels of being, and you have only accessed one. The kingdom is not visible from the level of ordinary consciousness. You have to be born into a new one.
