JESUSSAID

Luke 13:12-16

Daughter of Abraham

Woman, you are set free from your infirmity. You hypocrites! Doesn’t each of you on the Sabbath untie your ox or donkey from the stall and lead it out to give it water? Then should not this woman, a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan has kept bound for eighteen long years, be set free on the Sabbath day from what bound her?”

Setting a bound human being free is exactly what the sabbath is for; treating a rule as more sacred than a person is the real violation.

Context

A woman has been bent double for eighteen years. Jesus sees her in the synagogue, calls her over, and releases her — she straightens up and praises God. And the synagogue leader is indignant. Not at the suffering he has watched her carry for years. At the timing of the healing. There are six other days for work. Jesus's reply is one of the most exposing rhetorical questions in the gospels: you untie your ox on the sabbath to give it water — should this daughter of Abraham not be untied from what has bound her for eighteen years? The word for untie is the same word for what he did to her. He is drawing the parallel to their faces.
