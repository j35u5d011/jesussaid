Gospel of Thomas 108

Drink From My Mouth

Jesus said, "He who will drink from my mouth will become like me. I myself shall become he, and the things that are hidden will be revealed to him."

Whoever fully receives Jesus's teaching becomes like him and receives revelation of what is hidden.

Context

This is the talmid relationship spelled out in one line. In the first-century rabbinic world, a disciple did not just learn the rabbi's ideas — the disciple became the rabbi. Same walk, same speech, same responses to trouble. Jesus said it plainly in Matthew 10:24-25 and Luke 6:40: it is enough for the disciple to be like his teacher. John 13:15 — I have given you an example. Thomas 108 says the same thing in more intimate language. Whoever drinks from my mouth — receives the teaching directly, without filter, without dilution — becomes like me. And I myself shall become he. The two collapse into one shared reality.
