JESUSSAID

Matthew 15:32-34

I Have Compassion

I have compassion for these people; they have already been with me three days and have nothing to eat. I do not want to send them away hungry, or they may collapse on the way. How many loaves do you have?”

Jesus feeds another huge crowd because he refuses to let them go home empty, and the multiplication begins from what little his disciples can find.

Context

This is the second feeding in Matthew — four thousand this time, with seven loaves and a few small fish — and it is easy to read as a duplicate of the first. It is not. The location is Gentile territory, on the eastern side of the lake, and the number of baskets left over (seven, not twelve) may signal the mission reaching outward, beyond the twelve tribes. But the opening line is where the weight sits. I have compassion. Splanchnizomai in the Greek — moved in the gut. This is not a decision. This is a physical reaction to hunger in front of him. The kingdom is not run by calculation. It is run by mercy that will not let people be sent away hungry when there is any way to feed them.
