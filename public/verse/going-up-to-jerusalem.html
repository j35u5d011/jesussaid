Luke 18:31-33

We are going up to Jerusalem, and everything that is written by the prophets about the Son of Man will be fulfilled. He will be delivered over to the Gentiles. They will mock him, insult him and spit on him; they will flog him and kill him. On the third day he will rise again.”

Context

Three times in the synoptics Jesus tells the disciples what is coming. This is the third and most detailed. He names the mechanics — handed over to the chief priests, condemned, delivered to the Gentiles, mocked, flogged, crucified — and adds the resurrection on the third day. Whatever else is happening here, Jesus is not blindsided. He walks toward Jerusalem knowing the shape of what waits. Luke frames it as fulfillment of what the prophets wrote about the Son of Man. Mark and Matthew keep it lean and clinical. The disciples, as usual, do not understand — the very next scene in Matthew and Mark has them jockeying for thrones. They cannot hear what he is telling them because it does not fit the messiah they want.
