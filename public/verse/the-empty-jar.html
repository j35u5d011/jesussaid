Gospel of Thomas 97

The Empty Jar

Jesus said, "The kingdom of the father is like a certain woman who was carrying a jar full of meal. While she was walking on the road, still some distance from home, the handle of the jar broke and the meal emptied out behind her on the road. She did not realize it; she had noticed no accident. When she reached her house, she set the jar down and found it empty."

The kingdom can be lost quietly through inattention, without any dramatic failure, until you arrive home and find nothing left.

Context

This is one of Thomas's most disturbing parables, and it belongs beside Jesus's other warnings about inattention. A woman walks home from the mill with a jar of meal. The handle breaks. Meal trickles out behind her the whole way home. She notices nothing. She sets the jar down. It is empty. That is the whole story. No enemy stole from her. No storm broke the jar. Her own inattention did the work. When she finally looked, everything was gone.
