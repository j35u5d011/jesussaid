JESUSSAID

Luke 7:13-14

The Widow's Son at Nain

Don’t cry. Young man, I say to you, get up!”

Jesus stops a funeral procession and returns a widow's only son to her, moved by compassion for a mother about to lose everything.

Context

The scene is small and devastating. A widow is walking behind the body of her only son. In her world this means not just grief but destitution — she has no one left to provide for her. Jesus sees her and, Luke says, his heart went out to her. Compassion is the trigger. He speaks two words to her — don't cry — and two words to her dead son — get up. And the boy sits up. Jesus gives him back to his mother. There is no theology being taught here. No demand for faith. No parable attached. Just a widow he does not know, a stranger's son, and love that will not accept the ending the day was writing.
