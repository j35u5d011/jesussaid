JESUSSAID

John 12:44-50

Not to Judge, But to Save

Whoever believes in me does not believe in me only, but in the one who sent me. The one who looks at me is seeing the one who sent me. I have come into the world as a light, so that no one who believes in me should stay in darkness. If anyone hears my words but does not keep them, I do not judge that person. For I did not come to judge the world, but to save the world. There is a judge for the one who rejects me and does not accept my words; the very words I have spoken will condemn them at the last day. For I did not speak on my own, but the Father who sent me commanded me to say all that I have spoken. I know that his command leads to eternal life. So whatever I say is must what the Father has told me to say.”

Jesus does not judge those who reject him — the teaching itself is the judgment, activated by how you respond to it.

Context

Two lines in this passage cut against every image of Jesus as the future courtroom judge with a gavel. "I did not come to judge the world, but to save the world." And, "There is a judge for the one who rejects me — the very words I have spoken." The judgment is not something Jesus later delivers on us. It is something the teaching itself accomplishes, automatically, by how we respond to it. Same law he teaches everywhere. The measure you use is the measure used on you. What you do with the light determines whether you are walking in it or refusing it. The gavel is your own hand.
