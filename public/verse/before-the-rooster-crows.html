Context

Peter swears he will never fall away. Jesus knows better. Before the rooster crows you will disown me three times. It happens exactly as predicted. What makes the scene remarkable is not the failure — everyone in the story fails that night — but what Jesus does with the knowledge in advance. He does not disqualify Peter. In Luke he prays for him, tells him that when he has turned back, he is to strengthen his brothers. The stumbling is not the end. It is written into the story.
