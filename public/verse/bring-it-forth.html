Gospel of Thomas 70

Bring It Forth

Jesus said, "That which you have will save you if you bring it forth from yourselves. That which you do not have within you will kill you if you do not have it within you."

What lives within you must be brought out to save you, and what is not there cannot save you at all.

Context

This is one of Thomas's sharpest lines. There is no external rescue. Whatever will save you has to already be in you — and it has to be brought forth, expressed, lived, given away. Hidden, it does nothing. Undeveloped, it does nothing. The parable of the talents makes the same point (Mt 25:14-30) — the servant who buried his gift is the one who lost everything. The kingdom does not arrive from the sky. It rises from the inside and out into the world through your life. That is the whole shape of Jesus's way.
