JESUSSAID

Luke 20:46-47

Beware the Long Robes

Beware of the teachers of the law. They like to walk around in flowing robes and love to be greeted with respect in the marketplaces and have the most important seats in the synagogues and the places of honor at banquets. They devour widows’ houses and for a show make lengthy prayers. These men will be punished most severely.”

Religious leaders who use their office for status, wealth, and applause will be judged the hardest.

Context

This is short and Jesus doesn't soften it. The teachers of the law are performing — flowing robes for visibility, elaborate greetings in the marketplace, the best seats in the synagogue, the head of the table at banquets. Every detail is about being seen. And underneath the performance, they are eating widows out of house and home, then covering it with long public prayers. The prayers are not spiritual practice. They are marketing. Jesus reserves his hardest condemnation not for tax collectors or prostitutes but for religious professionals who monetize God.
