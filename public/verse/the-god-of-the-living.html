JESUSSAID

Mark 12:24-27

The God of the Living

Are you not in error because you do not know the Scriptures or the power of God? When the dead rise, they will neither marry nor be given in marriage; they will be like the angels in heaven. Now about the dead rising – have you not read in the Book of Moses, in the account of the burning bush, how God said to him, ‘I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob? He is not the God of the dead, but of the living. You are badly mistaken.”

The Sadducees miss the resurrection because they read the scriptures without understanding that God is a God of the living, not the dead.

Context

The Sadducees, who reject the resurrection, come with a trap dressed as a riddle. Seven brothers, one widow, whose wife is she in the afterlife? Jesus refuses the terms. You do not know the Scriptures or the power of God. The risen life is not a continuation of earthly arrangements. There is no marriage in the age to come — not because love ends, but because the categories that made marriage a legal and reproductive contract are no longer needed. People will be like the angels. The relational shape of eternity is not property and lineage. It is love without possession.
