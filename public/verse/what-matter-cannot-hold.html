JESUSSAID

Gospel of Mary 7:1-8:11

What Matter Cannot Hold

"Every nature, every form, every creature exists in and with each other, but they'll dissolve again into their own roots, because the nature of matter dissolves into its nature alone. Anyone who has ears to hear should hear! ... Sin doesn't exist, but you're the ones who make sin when you act in accordance with the nature of adultery, which is called 'sin.' That's why the Good came among you, up to the things of every nature in order to restore it within its root. ... That's why you get sick and die, because you love what tricks you. Anyone who can understand should understand! Matter gave birth to a passion that has no image because it came from what's contrary to nature. Then confusion arises in the whole body. That's why I told you to be content at heart. If you're discontented, find contentment in the presence of the various images of nature."

Every created form returns to its root, sin is not a substance but an action against nature, and inner contentment is the medicine.

Context

This is a rich teaching from the Savior in the Gospel of Mary — dialogue material recorded in the community around Mary Magdalene after the resurrection. The Savior begins with metaphysics. Every nature, every form, every creature holds together in mutual dependence and eventually dissolves back into its own root. Matter returns to matter. Nothing here is being demonized — this is simply how the material order works. Then the pivot that jumps off the page: sin does not exist. Not as a substance, not as a stain, not as a thing baked into you at birth. Sin is what you make when you act contrary to your true nature. It is a verb, not a noun. This is exactly the Jesus Way understanding of sin — harm you do, not a metaphysical debt you owe.
