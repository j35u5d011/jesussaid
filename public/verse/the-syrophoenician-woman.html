Mark 7:27-29

The Syrophoenician Woman

First let the children eat all they want, for it is not right to take the children’s bread and toss it to the dogs. For such a reply, you may go; the demon has left your daughter.”

A Gentile mother out-argues Jesus for her daughter's healing, and Jesus honours the exchange and heals.

Context

This is one of the strangest and most human scenes in the gospels. A Gentile woman begs Jesus for her daughter, and his first response is a hard word — let the children eat first; it is not right to take their bread and toss it to the dogs. She does not flinch. Lord, even the dogs under the table eat the children's crumbs. Something shifts. He answers: for such a reply, you may go — the demon has left your daughter. She has argued him back to the wider table that was always going to be part of his mission (Isa 56:7 — my house shall be a house of prayer for all nations).
