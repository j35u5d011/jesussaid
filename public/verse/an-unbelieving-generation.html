Luke 9:41

An Unbelieving Generation

You unbelieving and perverse generation, how long shall I stay with you and put up with you? Bring your son here.”

Jesus's frustration with the disciples' failure to heal is a call to deeper trust, not a rebuke of the boy or his father.

Context

Jesus comes down the mountain from the Transfiguration and finds the disciples defeated. A boy is convulsing. The father is desperate. They tried and could not help. Jesus's response has weary heat in it: unbelieving and perverse generation, how long shall I put up with you? Bring your son here. The scolding is aimed inward, at the disciples he has been teaching, not at the crowd or the father. He has shown them the kingdom's power at work. He has sent them out to do the same. And here they are, back in their old smallness. He takes over, heals the boy, and the story continues.
