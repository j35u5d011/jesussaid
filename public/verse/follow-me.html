JESUSSAID

John 1:43-51

Follow Me

Follow me. Here truly is an Israelite in whom there is no deceit. I saw you while you were still under the fig tree before Philip called you. You believe because I told you I saw you under the fig tree. You will see greater things than that. Very truly I tell you, you will see ‘heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending on’ the Son of Man.”

Discipleship begins with a simple call to follow, and the promise that you will see greater things by walking it.

Context

Jesus finds Philip and says two words — 'Follow me.' No sermon, no vetting, no theology. Philip goes and finds Nathanael and tells him they have found the one Moses and the prophets wrote about, Jesus of Nazareth. Nathanael's response is one of the most human lines in the gospels: 'Can anything good come from Nazareth?' Jesus doesn't correct him. When Nathanael approaches, Jesus sees straight through him — 'Here is a true Israelite in whom there is nothing false.' Nathanael, startled, asks how he could possibly know him. Jesus says, 'I saw you under the fig tree before Philip called you.' Nathanael, undone, declares him Son of God and King of Israel. Jesus, unimpressed by the quick conversion, says, 'You believe because I said that? You will see greater things than that.'
